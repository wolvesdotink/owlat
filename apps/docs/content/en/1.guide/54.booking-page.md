description: 'A personal page where people pick a time to meet you, with calendar invites, self-service cancel and reschedule, and your open times one click away in the composer.'
---

"When are you free?" usually costs three emails. A booking page ends that: you
set the hours you take meetings once, send a link, and the other person picks a
time that suits both of you. They get a calendar invite, and so do you.

::callout{type="info" title="Off by default"}
An admin turns it on under **Settings → Features → Booking page** (flag
`calendar.booking`, part of the Email client pack). Every member then gets
their own page.
::

## Set up your page

Open **My settings → Booking page**.

- **Link**: the address of your page, `https://<your Owlat>/book/<link>`. Use
  3 to 40 lowercase letters, digits and hyphens. Each link belongs to one
  person.
- **Name on the page**: what guests see. Empty means your profile name.
- **Your time zone**: the zone your hours are in. Guests always see times in
  their own zone.
- **Weekly hours**: one or more ranges per weekday. Two ranges leave a gap, for
  lunch for example. A day without ranges is not bookable.
- **Dates that differ**: replace a weekday's hours on one date, or leave the
  date without hours to take the day off. Dates that are over are removed when
  you save.
- **Rules**: the minimum notice (no bookings closer to now than this), how many
  days ahead people can book (up to 90), and a buffer kept free before and
  after every booking.

Then add one or more **meetings**: a title, a length, where it happens (an
office, a phone number) and, if you want, a video call link. Each meeting has
its own link, `/book/<your link>/<meeting>`. The video link is only sent to the
guest in their confirmation, never shown on the page. A paused meeting cannot
be booked; its link shows "not found" until you turn it back on.

## What your guest sees

The meeting page shows a month calendar with the days that still have open
times, and the times of the chosen day. Times are shown in the guest's time
zone, detected from their browser; they can switch it. After picking a time
they enter their name, email address and an optional note, and confirm.

Open times leave out everything already booked (plus your buffer), anything
sooner than your minimum notice, and anything past your booking horizon. Owlat
checks the time again when the booking is saved, so two people can never get
the same slot: whoever is second is asked to pick another time.

## The emails

Each booking sends two emails, one to the guest and one to you, both with an
iCalendar invite attached (`METHOD:REQUEST`), so the meeting lands in Google
Calendar, Outlook, Apple Calendar and the like.

- The guest's email comes from "_your name_ via Owlat" with **Reply-To set to
  you**, so a reply reaches you. It carries links to **pick another time** and
  to **cancel**.
- Your email has the guest's name, address and note, with Reply-To set to the
  guest.

A moved meeting sends an updated invite (the same event, so calendars move it
rather than adding a second one). A cancelled meeting sends a cancellation
(`METHOD:CANCEL`), which removes it from the calendars.

The emails go through your instance's system mail transport, the same one that
sends password resets, whether or not you have a mailbox in Owlat. When you
move a meeting, the guest's earlier links stop working; the newest email
always has the working ones.

## Your bookings

Upcoming bookings are listed at the top of **My settings → Booking page**, in
your browser's time zone. **Cancel meeting** cancels one and tells the guest.

## Insert availability

In the composer, **⋯ → Insert availability** adds your next five open times and
the link to your first active meeting to the message, after what you have
written and above your signature. The times are in your page's time zone. If
you have not set up a page yet, Owlat offers to take you there.

## Limits

The page is public, so it is held to a few limits:

- each guest address can hold at most three upcoming bookings with you;
- bookings from one network address, and bookings on one person's page, are
  rate limited;
- a hidden form field catches form-filling bots, which are told the booking
  worked while nothing is booked.

## Privacy

Your page, your meetings and every booking guests made are your personal data.
They appear in your [account export](/guide/account) and are deleted when your
account is deleted (the guests' names, addresses and notes with them). While
your account is being deleted, your page is no longer reachable.

## What it does not do yet

- It does not read your other calendars. Busy times are Owlat bookings only,
  so keep your weekly hours to times you really are free. (The
  [calendar feed for scheduling replies](/guide/calendar-availability) is not
  used for the booking page.)
- No team or round-robin pages, no payments, and no reminder emails before the
  meeting.
