---
title: "Brand Kit"
description: "Set your organization's logo, colors, fonts, button style and footer once, so every new email starts on-brand."
---

The brand kit is your organization's design system for email. It holds your logo, colors, fonts, button style and footer. Every new email starts from it, and the email editor offers it wherever you style a block, so your team does not have to rebuild the brand in each email.

## Opening the brand kit

Open **Settings > Workspace > Features > Brand kit**. Owners and admins can change it; everyone else sees its effect in the editor. The page shows a live preview beside the form: a sample email rendered by the same engine that sends your emails, in light and dark mode.

## What the brand kit holds

| Setting | What it controls | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Logo** | Shown at the top of new emails; insertable from the editor. Chosen from the [media library](/guide/media-library). | None |
| **Dark-mode logo** | Optional. Replaces the logo in email clients that switch to dark mode. | None |
| **Primary color** | Buttons, list bullets, social icons and other accents | `#c4785a` (terracotta) |
| **Secondary color** | Dividers and secondary accents | `#282d3a` |
| **Text color** | Headings and body text | `#374151` |
| **Background color** | The email body behind your content | `#ffffff` |
| **Link color** | Links inside text | `#2563eb` |
| **Extra swatches** | Up to six more colors for the editor's color pickers | None |
| **Heading and body font** | The typefaces of headings and of body text | Arial |
| **Button style** | Corner radius and padding of new buttons | 8 px radius, 24 × 12 px padding |
| **Email width** | Maximum width of your emails on desktop (400–800 px) | 600 px |
| **Footer** | Company name, postal address and social links | Empty |

Hex colors are checked before you can save, and **Reset to defaults** puts the defaults back into the form (nothing changes until you save).

### Fonts

The font lists have two groups. **Email-safe fonts** (Arial, Georgia, Verdana and others) look the same in every email client. **Web fonts** (Inter, Roboto, Open Sans, Lato, Montserrat, Poppins, Merriweather, Playfair Display, Lora) load from Google Fonts in clients that support it, such as Apple Mail, iOS Mail and Thunderbird. Gmail and Outlook do not load web fonts; they show the email-safe font each web font names as its fallback (Arial for the sans-serif fonts, Georgia for the serif ones).

### Footer

The footer holds your company name, postal address and social links. Many countries require a postal address in marketing emails, so it is worth filling in. If the company name is empty, the page offers your workspace name with one click.

The brand kit footer is separate from the unsubscribe footer that marketing emails always carry: that one is added at send time and cannot be removed.

## Import from website

**Import from website** reads your website and proposes a brand kit:

1. Enter your website's address, for example `example.com`.
2. Owlat fetches the page and up to three of its stylesheets. It takes the page's theme color, the colors its CSS uses most, its name, and logo candidates (the app icon, images marked as a logo, the site icon and the sharing image).
3. Review the proposal. Choose whether to use the colors and the name, and pick one logo candidate or keep the current logo.
4. **Apply to brand kit** fills the form. Only a logo you picked is downloaded, into your media library. Nothing else is saved until you save the brand kit.

Only public websites can be read: addresses that point into a private network are refused. Logos must be PNG, JPEG or SVG files without scripts.

## The brand kit in the editor

- **Brand colors first.** Every color picker in the editor shows your brand colors in their own row, before the preset and recent colors.
- **New blocks start on-brand.** Text, buttons, lists, menus, dividers, social icons and progress bars take the brand kit's colors, and new buttons its radius and padding. Headings and body text use the brand fonts.
- **New emails start with your logo and footer.** A blank email or campaign starts with the logo on top and the footer at the bottom. A template from the template library is restyled with your brand colors and fonts when it is created. Transactional emails start with the logo and the company details, without social links.
- **Insert the logo or footer anywhere.** Use the brand buttons in the block sidebar, or type `/logo` or `/footer`.
- **Apply brand kit.** The palette button in the editor's toolbar restyles an existing email: colors, fonts and button styles change to the brand kit. Text, images and links stay as they are, and so do colors set inside rich text. Text on dark sections keeps its color, so white text on a dark band stays readable. Blocks linked to the [saved block](/guide/saved-blocks) library are skipped. So is a block someone else is [editing at the same time](/guide/email-editor#editing-together) (it shows a lock); the confirmation tells you, and you can apply again once they are done. The whole change is one step you can undo.

## How the brand kit works with existing emails

The brand kit sets the starting point. Blocks carry their own colors, and a value set on a block always wins over the brand kit. Changing the brand kit therefore does not repaint emails that already exist; open them and use **Apply brand kit** to bring them in line.

What the brand kit changes directly in every email when you save it: the email background and width, link colors, and the fonts of text that has no font of its own.

::callout{type="tip"}
Set up the brand kit before you build your first templates. It is much easier to start on-brand than to restyle every email later.
::
