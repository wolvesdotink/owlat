A saved reply is an answer you write once and insert whenever the same question
comes in again: the refund policy, the opening hours, the "we got your message".
Saved replies work in every composer: the Postbox composer, Answer mode and the
Team Inbox reply.

## Inserting a saved reply

There are three ways in:

- **Type `;` and the shortcut.** At the start of a line or after a space, type
  `;` followed by the reply's shortcut (`;refund`). A list of matching replies
  opens under the cursor; **Enter** or **Tab** inserts the highlighted one and
  **Esc** closes the list. The match is fuzzy, so `;rfnd` finds `refund` too.
  The Postbox composer still accepts `/`, which it used before saved replies.
- **The picker.** The speech-bubble button beside **Send**, or **Ctrl+;**
  (**⌘;** on a Mac), opens a searchable list of every reply you can use, the
  ones you use most at the top. Arrow keys move, **Enter** inserts.
- **The command palette.** While you are writing, **⌘K** / **Ctrl+K** lists your
  saved replies under **Saved replies**.

The reply goes in where the cursor was. In the Team Inbox, where replies are
plain text, its formatting is turned into plain text.

## Variables and gaps

A saved reply can contain variables that are filled in when you insert it:

| Variable                | Becomes                                             |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| `{{contact.firstName}}` | The recipient's first name                          |
| `{{contact.lastName}}`  | The recipient's last name                           |
| `{{contact.email}}`     | The recipient's email address                       |
| `{{me.firstName}}`      | Your first name                                     |
| `{{me.name}}`           | Your full name                                      |
| `{{thread.subject}}`    | The conversation's subject, without `Re:` or `Fwd:` |
| `{{today}}`             | Today's date, in your language                      |

In the Postbox the recipient is the first **To** address, with the name from
your address book; in the Team Inbox it is the thread's contact. A variable can
carry its own fallback, written `{{contact.firstName|'there'}}`.

A variable without a value never goes out as `{{…}}`. It becomes a **gap**:
highlighted text in double brackets such as `[[Recipient’s first name]]`. You
can also write gaps into a saved reply yourself, for what you fill in by hand
every time: `Your order [[order number]] ships today.` While a gap is left in
the reply, **Send** waits and the composer says how many are left, also after
you save the reply as a draft and open it again later. In the Postbox, clicking
a gap selects it, so you can type over it.

Other `{{tokens}}` you write into a reply are listed under **Variables** in its
editor, where you can say what each one means or have the composer ask for it
when you insert the reply.

## Saving a reply

- **From the composer:** open the picker and choose **Save as reply**, or pick
  **Save as reply** in the command palette. What you have written is saved
  (without your signature or the quoted message), under the name and shortcut
  you give it.
- **From the settings:** **My settings → Saved replies** lists your personal
  replies, with how often and when you last used each, and lets you add, change
  and delete them.

A shortcut is one word: spaces become dashes and it is saved in lower case.

## Personal and shared replies

A **personal** reply is yours alone and shows in every composer you write in.

A **shared** reply belongs to the team and shows in every member's picker.
Owners and admins manage shared replies under **Admin → Team → Saved replies**
(and can choose **Everyone in the team** when saving from a composer). A shared
reply can be limited to some [team inboxes](/guide/postbox); it then shows only
when you write from one of them, and not in the Team Inbox. Everyone else can
insert shared replies but not change them.

Each insert counts towards the reply's use, which orders the picker. A shared
reply's count is the whole team's.

## Your data

Your personal saved replies are part of your [data export](/guide/account) and
are deleted with your account. Shared replies stay with the team when an
account is deleted; only the name of who wrote them is removed.

## Upgrading from snippets

Saved replies replace the Postbox's per-mailbox snippets, and every snippet is
kept. A snippet in your own mailbox is now one of your personal replies; a
snippet in a team inbox is now a shared reply limited to that inbox, which only
admins can change. Snippet bodies that use `{{firstName}}` keep working.
