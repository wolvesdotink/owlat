This page is a reference for the per-user behavior settings under **Postbox → Settings**. They're preferences of the person, not of a mailbox, so they apply across every mailbox you have on this instance. Most live in the settings page's **Reading** section; a couple sit in their own cards below it. The feature pages ([Postbox](/guide/postbox), [Smart Inbox](/guide/smart-inbox), [Reply Queue](/guide/reply-queue)) explain the behavior in context — this is the flat list of switches.

Changes save the moment you flip them.

## Reading

| Setting                                   | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Default                    |
| ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| **After archiving, deleting or snoozing** | What the reader shows once you triage the open conversation away — **Open the next conversation**, **Open the previous conversation**, or **Go back to the list**. See [auto-advance](/guide/postbox#auto-advance-after-triage).   | Open the next conversation |
| **Density**                               | How tightly the message list and reader are packed: **Comfortable** (roomy) or **Compact** (tighter rows, single-line subject/snippet).                                                                                            | Comfortable                |
| **Default reply behavior**                | What the **Reply** button and the `r` shortcut do — **Reply to the sender only** or **Reply to everyone (reply all)**. The `a` shortcut always replies to everyone, and you can switch a reply to reply-all while composing.       | Reply to the sender only   |
| **Writing suggestions**                   | Inline AI ghost-text autocomplete as you write; press **Tab** to accept. Only shown when the **`ai`** flag is on — this toggle is the per-user opt-out within an AI-enabled instance.                                              | On (when `ai` is enabled)  |
| **Show overviews**                        | Open personal threads on their AI Overview (latest update, open items, activity) instead of the raw conversation. Only shown when the **`ai`** flag is on. People who had auto-summaries off keep the conversation.                | On (when `ai` is enabled)  |
| **Open threads on**                       | Whether a personal thread opens on its **Overview** or its **Conversation**. The switch in the reader still changes one thread. Shown while overviews are on.                                                                      | Overview                   |
| **Prepare overviews for recent mail**     | Reads your active conversations from the last 30 days so they open on an Overview. It uses the instance's AI budget, pauses when that is spent, and resumes where it stopped. Older threads are prepared when you first open them. | Not started                |
| **Play sound when sending**               | Play a short confirmation sound when a message is dispatched.                                                                                                                                                                      | Off                        |

::callout{type="note" title="The AI toggles need the `ai` flag"}
**Writing suggestions** and **Show overviews** only appear when the instance has the **`ai`** feature flag enabled (with an LLM provider configured). The flag is the master switch; these toggles let an individual user opt out. See [Feature flags](/guide/feature-flags).
::

## On this device

The **On this device** card controls the optional offline read cache — a small, device-local, unencrypted copy of your recent mail so Postbox opens instantly and stays readable offline. Mail you send while offline is also stored on this device (queued, then sent automatically on reconnect); the queue lives in the same local database but has no toggle of its own — a send must never silently vanish. Full details in [Postbox → Offline & local cache](/guide/postbox#offline-local-cache).

| Setting                              | What it does                                                                                             | Default                      |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| **Store recent mail on this device** | Keep the newest inbox rows and recently-read bodies (already sanitized) in this device's local database. | On (wiped when you sign out) |
| **Clear local cache**                | Wipe this device's cached mail immediately. Not a toggle — a button.                                     | —                            |

::callout{type="warning" title="Leave the cache off on shared computers"}
The on-device cache is unencrypted local storage. Turn **Store recent mail on this device** off (or clear it) on any machine you don't control.
::

## Notifications (desktop app only)

The **Notifications** card only appears in the [desktop app](/guide/desktop-app), where Postbox can fire native OS notifications and drive the dock/taskbar unread badge.

| Setting                         | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Default                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Notify me about**             | Which new inbox mail pops a native notification — **Everything**, **People & important only**, or **Nothing**. "People & important only" uses [Smart Inbox](/guide/smart-inbox) categories to stay quiet about newsletters and automated mail (mail not yet classified still notifies, so nothing is dropped before the classifier runs). | People & important only when smart categories are live (the `ai` flag is on); otherwise Everything |
| **Count all mail in the badge** | Whether non-people mail still increments the dock/taskbar unread badge. Turn it off to keep the badge count focused on people & important mail even while notifications are quiet.                                                                                                                                                        | On                                                                                                 |

## Related settings pages

A few Postbox settings live on their own pages rather than as simple toggles:

- **Saved replies** — your personal [saved replies](/guide/saved-replies), inserted with `;` and a shortcut.
- **Writing voice** (needs `ai`) — teach AI drafts to [sound like you](/guide/postbox#writing-voice-write-in-my-voice), learned from your sent mail.
- **Signatures**, **Filters**, **Aliases**, **Forwarding**, **Vacation**, **App passwords** — covered in the [Postbox guide](/guide/postbox).
